## What the company makes

- Proton therapy system (质子治疗系统) — the first domestically developed proton therapy
  device in China. Approved by the National Medical Products Administration under the
  innovative medical device pathway on 2022-09-26; in clinical service at the Tumor Proton
  Center of Ruijin Hospital (Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine) since
  November 2023.
- Accelerator system — injector, low-energy transport line, synchrotron main accelerator,
  high-energy beam transport line.
- 180-degree rotating-gantry treatment room — proton IMPT with respiratory gating.
- 360-degree rotating-gantry treatment room — CBCT 3D positioning, AI respiratory gating.
- Horizontal fixed-beam treatment room — pencil-beam scanning, kV X-ray image guidance,
  robotic treatment couch.

Stated by the company: 67 core patents, roughly CNY 1 billion cumulative R&D investment,
and close to 1,400 patients treated across more than 50 tumor types as of the About page
read on 2026-09-14.
